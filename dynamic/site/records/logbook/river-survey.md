---
title: River survey, upper Min valley
date: 2026-03-14
summary: Two days of transects along the river; bamboo cover and three fresh scat sites.
---

We walked the eastern bank first, then crossed at the old ford. Bamboo density was
higher than the 2025 survey along the second transect, and we logged three fresh scat
sites within 400 m of the water.

Weather held until the afternoon of day two. Camera traps 4 and 7 were retrieved;
trap 7 had one clear daytime pass.
