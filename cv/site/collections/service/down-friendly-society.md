---
title: Founder and Treasurer
organization: Down Friendly Society (parish mutual-aid association)
start: 1850
end: 1877
order: 6
---

A local charitable mutual-aid society Darwin founded and ran
for nearly thirty years to provide sickness insurance for
labourers in the parish of Downe. Treasurer throughout. An
example of the quiet local civic work that ran in parallel with
Darwin's scientific career.
