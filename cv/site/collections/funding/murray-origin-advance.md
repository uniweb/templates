Advance from the publisher John Murray on the first edition of
*On the Origin of Species*. The entire print run of 1,250 copies
sold out on the day of publication (24 November 1859) and a second
edition was in press within weeks. Advances on subsequent editions
and on *The Descent of Man*, *The Expression of the Emotions*, and
the plant monographs followed the same arrangement.
