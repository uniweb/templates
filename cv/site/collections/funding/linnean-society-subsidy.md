Small publication subsidy supporting the joint communication of
Darwin's and Wallace's papers on the transmutation of species,
read before the Linnean Society on 1 July 1858 by Lyell and
Hooker and published in the society's *Journal* later that year.
Covered the costs of extracts, offprints, and dissemination.
