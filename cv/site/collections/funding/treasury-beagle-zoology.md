Grant from the Treasury, recommended to Parliament by the Chancellor of
the Exchequer on application from naturalists including Buckland and
Peacock, to defray the cost of illustration and publication of the
zoological specimens collected on the second voyage of H.M.S. Beagle.
Distributed across five volumes edited by Darwin and prepared by
specialist contributors (Owen, Waterhouse, Gould, Jenyns, Bell).
