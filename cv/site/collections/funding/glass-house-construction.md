Construction of a heated glass-house at Down House to support
controlled observations of insectivorous and climbing plants.
The facility enabled the experimental work reported in
*Insectivorous Plants* (1875), *The Movements and Habits of
Climbing Plants* (1875), and *The Power of Movement in Plants* (1880).
Maintained and expanded over two decades with additional
personal outlays for heating, watering, and specimen collection.
