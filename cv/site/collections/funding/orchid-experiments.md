Travel, specimen purchase, and hot-house cultivation supporting
the pollination studies reported in *On the Various Contrivances
by which British and Foreign Orchids are Fertilised by Insects*
(1862). Field observations on *Orchis*, *Ophrys*, and *Epipactis*
across Kent; tropical species raised at Down House from Kew
specimens supplied by Sir Joseph Hooker.
