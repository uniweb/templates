Grant from the Royal Society's fund for scientific investigation,
applied to a programme of continuous observation at Down House on
the action of earthworms in the formation of vegetable mould.
Included field measurements in surrounding pastures, controlled
observations on a measured plot in the garden, and the weighed
castings used to estimate worm activity per acre. Reported in
*The Formation of Vegetable Mould through the Action of Worms* (1881).
