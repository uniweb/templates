start: 1853

Shaped the public reception of *Origin* as "Darwin's Bulldog",
particularly in the Oxford evolution debate of 1860. Exchanged
detailed letters on comparative anatomy, invertebrate morphology,
and on the classification of the Cirripedia. Frequent house guest
at Down and a trusted reader of drafts.
