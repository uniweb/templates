Forty years of sustained correspondence and collaboration. Hooker was
the first naturalist Darwin confided in about transmutation of species
(January 1844 letter), later supplied tropical orchid specimens from
Kew for the pollination studies, and remained Darwin's closest
scientific confidant and critic throughout his career.
