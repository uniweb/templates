title: Edward Bagnall Poulton
relationship: Correspondent (younger generation)
topic: Mimicry and protective colouration
start: 1877
end: 1882
order: 6
---

Exchanged letters in the final five years on experimental tests
of the mimicry hypothesis in Lepidoptera. Poulton went on to
become one of the most prominent defenders of natural selection
in the late Victorian period and wrote Darwin's biography for the
Royal Society.
