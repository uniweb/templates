title: Francis Darwin

topic: Plant physiology and movement
start: 1875

order: 4
---

Francis served as Darwin's research assistant for the plant-studies
programme during his father's final years and co-authored *The Power
of Movement in Plants* (1880). Conducted the circumnutation
measurements and the light-response experiments on grass coleoptiles.
