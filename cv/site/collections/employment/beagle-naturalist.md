title: Naturalist, H.M.S. Beagle
role: Naturalist
organization: H.M.S. Beagle (second voyage, under Captain Robert FitzRoy)
location: South America, Galápagos Islands, Tahiti, New Zealand, Australia, South Africa
start: 1831
end: 1836
order: 2
---

Five-year circumnavigation collecting zoological, botanical, geological,
and ethnographic specimens. Fieldwork across Patagonia, Tierra del Fuego,
the Andes, the Galápagos archipelago, and coral atolls of the Pacific
and Indian Oceans. The observations laid the foundation for subsequent
work on the transmutation of species and the formation of coral reefs.
