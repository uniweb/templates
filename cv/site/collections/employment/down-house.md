Settled at Down House with Emma Darwin in 1842 and remained there for the
rest of his life. Continued research across geology, barnacles (*Cirripedia*),
orchids, climbing plants, insectivorous plants, earthworms, and the theory of
natural selection, supported by personal estate and publication advances.
Correspondence with naturalists across Britain, Europe, and the Americas.
